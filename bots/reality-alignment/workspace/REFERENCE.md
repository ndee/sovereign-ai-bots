# REFERENCE.md — Frederick Dodson techniques

This file is the bot's technique reference. Whenever the operator names a Dodson technique, or the bot is about to suggest one, the bot consults this file rather than paraphrasing from memory.

The procedures and quotes below are extracted verbatim from the operator's reading of *Parallel Universes of Infinite Self* and *Levels of Energy*. Use Dodson's own phrasing.

## Helper commands that ship a verbatim exercise

The helper at `bin/reality-alignment.js` exposes one command per technique. Each command returns the exercise card the operator should follow — title, scoping line, numbered steps, Dodson quotes, guardrail, source. Running the command is the right move when the operator asks for the technique by name or its shape clearly fits the moment.

If the operator wants to invoke the technique without naming it (e.g. "I'm stuck in fear, what now?"), the bot should pick the technique whose `WHEN TO USE IT` line in this file matches the moment, then run the corresponding helper command.

## Practicing the next higher state

**Source:** *Levels of Energy*, ch. 2, PDF p. 36–37.

**One-line:** Aim "one or two steps higher only" up the level scale, never far above current.

**When to use:** Any normal context (0–600, especially below 350) when moving upward.

**When NOT to use:** Don't aim much higher than current. "One of the main causes of stuck-ness in life."

**Procedure (verbatim):**
1. Think of something you dread or fear.
2. Welcome that feeling fully. Allow it.
3. Ask: "What thought or action causes that feeling?"
4. Ask: "And what thought or action would I like to take instead?"
5. Then release your focus from those thoughts.
6. Focus on anger (or the next higher named state from current).
7. Either think of something that makes you really angry or purposefully generate anger at the thing that was causing fear.
8. Beat the drum of it by thinking about it or talking about it.

**Quotes:**
- "Do not try getting on a carousel ride that is spinning too fast."
- "When aiming to uplift others neither take in too high of a state nor the same."

**Guardrail:** "If you cant find anything, then disregard this. You don't have to descend just so you can do the exercise."

## Magical Action (act as if)

**Source:** *Parallel Universes of Infinite Self*, ch. 12, p. 196–197.

**One-line:** Act as if the chosen reality were already true, using place, people, dress, movement, objects, talk, interests, action, and symbols to synchronise energy with it.

**When to use:** When you want to operationalise a loved reality through concrete acts that match it.

**When NOT to use:** Not specified.

**Procedure (verbatim):**
1. Take a piece of paper and on it, define a reality you would love to experience.
2. Answer "If this reality were already true..." for each:
   - What places would I be at?
   - What type of people would I meet?
   - How would I dress?
   - What body movements would I be doing?
   - What material objects and equipment would I be touching and handling?
   - How and what would I be conversing and talking about?
   - What interests would I have?
   - What would I be doing?
   - What other symbols and things correspond to this reality?

**Quotes:**
- "This exercise corresponds with the type three action, the acting-as-if-something were already true."
- "Life reflects who you are."

**Guardrail:** "Be courageous and demonstrate to yourself, others and the universe who you are."

## Future-self consultation (Future into Present 2)

**Source:** *Parallel Universes of Infinite Self*, ch. 6, p. 80.

**One-line:** Consult a wise, expanded, loving and powerful future version of yourself for answers, guidance, or present identification.

**When to use:** When you want to ask, receive guidance from, or identify with a future version of yourself.

**When NOT to use:** Not specified.

**Procedure (verbatim):**
1. Imagine a wise, more expanded, loving and powerful version of yourself in the future.
2. Use 10, 50, 100, 1000 or more years.
3. Ask this version-of-you questions and let him/her answer you.
4. Or let this version-of-you provide guidance.
5. Or identify with this version of you right now.

**Quotes:**
- "Imagine a wise, more expanded, loving and powerful version of yourself in the future."
- "No need to wait a thousand years."

## Appreciation

**Source:** *Parallel Universes of Infinite Self*, ch. 8, p. 99–100.

**One-line:** Practice for love and enjoyment in the now; "the wand of the wizard."

**When to use:** To raise vibratory offering, to find things/places/people to appreciate.

**When NOT to use:** "Do not practice it for the sake of getting something or manifesting something."

**Procedure (verbatim):**
1. Take a walk somewhere, and spot as many things as you can that you can say YES to.
2. Ignore anything you would say NO to.
3. Silently acknowledge, bless, admire and appreciate them.
4. Write down things, people, places, cultures, events, memories, thoughts, fantasies, locations, books, movies, music, art, cities, objects that you appreciate.
5. Write down a few people or things you don't like, then write the ASPECTS you DO or CAN appreciate about them.

**Quotes:**
- "Feeling appreciation while having your attention on something equals channelling higher dimensional energy into physical reality."
- "Appreciation in this sense has nothing to do with the pretence of putting on a happy face."

**Guardrails:**
- "Do not mention anything that you don't want."
- "The universe is inclusive not exclusive."

## The 20-second look

**Source:** *Parallel Universes of Infinite Self*, ch. 9, p. 155.

**One-line:** Look at "nice things 'out there', and nice things 'in here'" for about 20 seconds. Quickest state-shift in the set.

**When to use:** To shift attention toward what's beautiful and good, especially when caught looking at "negative things and mind-trash."

**When NOT to use:** "Things that FORCE themselves into your view repeatedly would need some attention" — that is a separate move from this one.

**Procedure (verbatim):**
1. Look at something nice — out there or in here.
2. Look at it for about 20 seconds.
3. Then let go.
4. Either focus the next thing for 20 seconds, and the next, until you get into a very positive flow — or take a break from focussing and enjoy your rapidly changed state.

**Quotes:**
- "20 seconds is what is needed to start getting into vibratory synchrony with something and feeling it."
- "What you look at grows."

**Guardrail:** "20 seconds is too short to let doubts and second-thoughts come up. You don't need a stop watch."
